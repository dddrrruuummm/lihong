# Lihong Global Trading Limited

## Art direction

A gem tray becomes the page composition. Oversized ivory and champagne serif type introduces the three business categories against a full-width velvet backdrop. A wide necklace photograph below opens onto a shell-paper collection spread. Gallery captions pair each design with a reference and a link that preselects it in the trade brief. The original facet mark is cut from the geometry of a jewel's setting. Wine surfaces, gold highlights, and ruled detail rows extend the visual language to the inquiry and company pages.

The Taste Skill specimen catalogue draw informs the collection groupings, scale changes and direct captions. The intense jewelry imagery called for a restrained wine, shell and champagne system in place of the generic draw palette. Newsreader and Onest are self-hosted. The workspace Apple, Ferrari and Wired analyses informed object-first photography, disciplined accent use and editorial pacing. Impeccable informed the visual hierarchy, keyboard states, responsive layout and contact flow. The opening differs from SALDY1209's dark split hero and Robert Cook Goods' blue four-image contact sheet.

## Images and interaction

Nine original high-jewelry images provide a dramatic cover and eight distinct gallery designs. The Craft & Packaging page adds three gift-box concepts with the full company name in gold foil and three workshop illustrations. Gift-box frames preserve the complete composition so the name stays visible. The imagery conveys design concepts and techniques. See [image notes](docs/IMAGERY.md). The trade brief works locally with labeled controls and a text fallback. The implementation has no decorative motion. Desktop and phone layouts retain the same information order with different image framing.
